## Three Key Ideas

### 1. The Ket Is a State Projection — and That Is a Structural Choice

The quantum ket |ψ⟩ is not a neutral representation of a measurement. It is a **projection** — a compression of a finite, historically embedded measurement event into a stateless symbol. The projection operator is 𝒫^Q: N_α(M_t) → |ψ⟩. In making this move, the ket discards the measurement's uncertainty, provenance, and history. Geofinitism classifies this as the **Ket Limit**: N_α(M_t) ≢ |ψ⟩. The ket is useful — but it is a useful fiction operating beyond its first-order authority.

### 2. The Dynexil Performs Trajectory Projection

The Dynexil responds to the Ket Limit with a different projection strategy:

> 𝔛_α^{(k,τ)}(M_t) = [N_α(M_t), N_α(M_{t-τ}), ..., N_α(M_{t-kτ})]_{U,P}

Instead of compressing a measurement into a state-symbol, the Dynexil retains a **local history** of k Nexils at delay τ, with uncertainty and provenance preserved throughout. This is **trajectory projection** (𝒫^T), not state projection (𝒫^Q). The result is a local, bounded, dynamical symbolic construct — not a global state claim.

### 3. Symbols Are Not Independent — They Are Trajectory-Embedded

A hidden assumption in many formal systems is that measurement symbols can be treated as statistically independent draws. ATT_74 rejects this:

> s_t ~ F(s_t, s_{t-τ}, s_{t-2τ}, ..., U_α, P_M)

Each symbol is a function of its local history, uncertainty, and provenance. The Dynexil makes this dependency explicit, connecting to delay embedding theory (Γ_α(t) = [N_α(M_t), ..., N_α(M_{t-kτ})]) while extending it to carry full symbolic accountability: **[·]_{U,P}** preserves uncertainty and provenance across the entire embedding window.

## Five Reflection Questions

1. **The Ket Limit** states N_α(M_t) ≢ |ψ⟩. In your own words, what does the ket compress out of a Nexil — and why does that matter for dynamical systems?

2. The Dynexil uses parameters k (embedding depth) and τ (symbolic delay). What happens to the Dynexil as k → 0? What does a Dynexil with k = 0 look like, and how does it compare to the Nexil N_α(M_t)?

3. ATT_74 claims that s_t ≢ an independent symbol. Identify a domain outside physics (e.g., language, economics, biology) where treating measurement symbols as independent is a standard assumption. What would change if a Dynexil-style framework were applied?

4. The Slow Nouns metaphor: "The ket is a noun. The Dynexil is a slowed trajectory." What does it mean for a trajectory to be *slowed enough* to function as a symbol? Where does the Alphonic Limit appear in this slowing process?

5. ATT_74 proposes that the Dynexil is applicable to **decoherence** in quantum systems. If decoherence is modelled as trajectory degradation — loss of Nexil coherence across the embedding window — how would you detect this in a Dynexil representation? What signature would you look for in the uncertainty bundle [·]_U?
