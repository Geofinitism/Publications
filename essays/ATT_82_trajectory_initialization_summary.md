## Core Claim

Every non-trivial functional symbolic trajectory must be explicitly initialised before substantive motion begins. This explicit initialisation is the **safety layer** — the minimal act of intellectual honesty required once symbols are treated as finite, measured objects rather than timeless Platonic entities. The compact initialisation form is:

**(C, α, H, δ)**

where C is the consensus/admissibility constraint, α is the Alphonic (resolution/scale) limit, H is the historical/procedural provenance, and δ is the declared uncertainty. Without this declaration, both human and artificial reasoners are forced into *symbolic mind-reading* — assuming that incoming symbols already carry their necessary scaffolding, which they frequently do not.

## Argument

### Point of Departure

ATT_82 is the prescriptive companion to ATT_81's descriptive account. ATT_81 introduced the FST as the working unit of language understood as a nonlinear dynamical system: a finite, stabilised pathway carrying representation, constraint, local uncertainty, and history through use — a dynamic object that must be generated, followed, tested, remembered, and allowed to fail within semantic phase space. ATT_82 draws the operational consequence:

> "Once functional symbolic trajectories are recognised as the actual medium in which all thought and communication unfold, a non-negotiable discipline becomes visible: every non-trivial trajectory must be explicitly initialised before substantive motion begins."

### Symbolic Mind-Reading

Without explicit initialisation, the receiver — human or artificial — must assume that the incoming symbols carry the necessary scaffolding: the precise definition of "solution," the measurement regime, the admissible uncertainty, the historical provenance, the constraint structure. When these are absent or only partially declared, the trajectory is launched on undeclared assumptions. The essay gives the failure modes across three domains:

- **Human conversation**: produces misunderstanding
- **Mathematical reasoning**: produces elegant but incomplete formulations  
- **LLM interactions**: produces confident answers that rest on sand — "error that feels complete"

The last is the most practically dangerous. Current LLMs are optimised for fluency within whatever frame the user supplies. A weak or underdeclared frame generates a coherent, authoritative-looking response that inherits every hidden commitment the user failed to state. The result is not identifiable as error; it feels complete.

### The Initialisation Tuple

The safety layer is formalised as the declaration of **(C, α, H, δ)** before substantive motion:

- **C** — consensus or admissibility constraint: what counts as valid within this trajectory
- **α** — the Alphonic limit: the resolution and scale at which symbols are being stabilised
- **H** — historical and procedural provenance: how the trajectory was generated and what has been compressed or omitted
- **δ** — the uncertainty: the declared neighbourhood of possible reconstructions

This is not bureaucratic overhead. It is the minimal act required once we refuse to treat symbols as timeless objects. It forces the writer or prompter to answer: *"Under what conditions will this trajectory be considered stabilised? What measurement boundary am I accepting? What hidden commitments am I asking the reader or model to supply through mind-reading?"*

### Three Levels of Protection

The safety layer operates at three interlocking levels:

**1. Trajectory integrity.** An explicitly initialised FST cannot quietly slip its constraints or drift into a nearby attractor without the drift being visible against the declared frame. Incoherence becomes detectable rather than camouflaged.

**2. Receiver protection.** The LLM, the student, the collaborator, or the theorem-prover is no longer required to guess the intended container. The frame is supplied; the geodesic can be followed or challenged on declared terms.

**3. The possibility of genuine progress.** Many of the deepest open questions in mathematics and science — P vs. NP named explicitly — have remained unresolved not because the symbolic machinery is insufficient, but because the initial framing has been allowed to remain partially undeclared. The classical formulation compresses a vast set of measurement commitments into compact, idealised language. That compression is a strength inside its own basin; it becomes a limitation the moment we insist on treating computation as a measured, finite process. Explicit initialisation restores the care required to write the problem down honestly.

### The LLM Case

The practical application is stated with precision:

> "The first response of a properly disciplined system would be: 'Before proceeding, here is the functional symbolic trajectory I am initialising. These are the constraints, the resolution, the provenance, and the declared uncertainty. Does this match the trajectory you intend?'"

This is not a call for pedantry but for gravity. The refusal to initialise is no longer a neutral omission once language is understood as real-time generation and navigation of functional symbolic trajectories. It is the precise point at which coherence can fail, where hidden assumptions masquerade as shared understanding, and where the measurement boundary is quietly crossed without acknowledgment.

### Self-Application

The essay closes with a reflexive move: the essays in the volume are themselves written under explicit initialisation. The reader is invited to treat every chapter not as a collection of fixed claims but as a set of explicitly framed functional symbolic trajectories. Where the frame is declared, the geodesic can be followed with confidence. Where it is not, the invitation is open: ask for the initialisation, and the safety layer will be supplied.
